How the model works

Throughput

The normal labor rate is adjusted for heavy freight and a planning reserve. Capacity is the smallest of available labor capacity, door capacity, and belt capacity. Dedicated support must be covered before unloading starts.

Time and cost

Finish time equals volume divided by running throughput, plus a fixed lost-time allowance. Crew cost assumes everyone stays paid until completion. It excludes overtime premiums and does not imply verified savings.

Scope

Late trailers are represented by available doors and equivalent lost minutes. This model does not schedule individual trailer arrivals, model breaks separately, or simulate congestion. Replace sample rates with observed sustained rates before operational use.
