The management decision

The full sample shows that Load loses a larger share of paid hours to interruptions and has more quality errors per 10,000 packages. Its productive-time throughput is closer to target. Investigate the interruption causes and quality before adding headcount; extra people may spend more time waiting too.

A practical pilot would log interruption causes, trial one process change on comparable shifts, and track utilization, quality, completion time, and throughput. This is a hypothesis from fictional data; no real savings or completed pilot are claimed.

Definitions and limitations

PPH means packages per hour. Paid hours include modeled interruption time; productive hours exclude it. All ratios use summed values, not averages of percentages. Target attainment compares packages with the sum of each area's target rate × productive hours. Error rates describe fictional quality events. The single-table model has one row per date and area. There are no live integrations, payroll savings, or employer records.
