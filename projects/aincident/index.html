01 / PROBLEM STATEMENT

Turn an incident into a usable report.

Retail teams need to turn the details of a security or operational incident into a clear, usable record. The reporting workflow must support the person documenting the event and the manager reviewing it, while producing a report that can be shared outside the system. The product challenge was to connect incident capture, written summaries, controlled access, and report export in one application.

AIncident addressed that workflow with AI-assisted incident reporting. The goal was to make the path from incident details to a reviewable report more direct.

02 / MY RESPONSIBILITY

Own the product from requirements to sale.

As founder and lead developer of Security Flaw Solutions, I translated the reporting workflow into an application, built the software, deployed it, and brought it to market. My work covered the reporting experience, AI integration, access controls, report output, and billing.
