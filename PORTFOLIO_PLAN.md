# Operations technology portfolio plan

## 1. Establish technical evidence

Publish the sort planner and dispatch dashboard with transparent sample data, readable models, calculation tests, working controls, and project READMEs. Keep the existing portfolio and career history as the context for the projects. Demonstrate the operational decision first, then the implementation.

## 2. Complete AIncident evidence

Add only owner-approved original screenshots/demo material and code still owned. Explain the user problem, requirements, two or three actual tradeoffs, delivery, and handoff. Retain the documented commercial sale as the outcome; do not add unsupported usage or efficiency claims. This stage depends on the owner's material.

## 3. Validate with operators

Ask a sort supervisor to review assumptions and a dispatcher to label sample exceptions. Record feedback, changes, and unresolved limitations. Propose a small observational pilot; measure prediction error and quality for staffing, and false alarms and review time for dispatch. Label any pilot as proposed until it happens.

## 4. Prepare three short interview walkthroughs

- Sort planner: show the baseline; reduce belt capacity; explain why more crew cannot overcome a physical bottleneck; compare the revised assignment and assigned hours.
- Dispatch dashboard: show the priority queue; change tolerance; explain a missing-scan flag; acknowledge it and explain why acknowledgement does not resolve risk.
- AIncident: explain the actual problem, personal responsibility, a confirmed design decision, delivery, and sale. Use authorized original evidence when available.

## 5. Use the portfolio in applications

Lead with operational experience plus the ability to turn requirements into working tools. Link directly to a relevant project in applications for product, business systems, and operations-technology roles. Keep simulated project metrics separate from career outcomes. Evidence of organizational product leadership remains a separate development goal for later director roles.

## Suggested next iteration

Add import validation for anonymized operator data, sensitivity comparisons, and documented user feedback after the initial demos have been reviewed. Prioritize these from real feedback instead of adding features solely for portfolio size.
